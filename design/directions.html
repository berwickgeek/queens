Queens: pick a direction

Same 8x8 puzzle, part-solved (three queens placed, a few cells crossed out), in three looks. Each one picks a different spot for the whimsy. Pick one, or say what to take from each.

A. Garden party

Soft pastel flower beds on a cool, pale-green ground with a deep leaf-green ink. Rounded type (SF Rounded on the phone), rounded cells, lots of air. Whimsy: each queen is a little crown that drops in with a small bounce, and when you solve the board a flower blooms from every queen and drifts off. Gentle and friendly. It gives up some of the crispness you'd want at 11x11, where 121 small rounded cells look busy.

B. Stained glass

Each region is a pane of coloured glass set in dark navy leading, on a cool grey-blue page. Serif level titles (New York on the phone), plain sans for the rest. Whimsy: placed queens catch the light with a soft glint, and a solved board gets a slow band of sunlight sweeping across it. Region borders are easiest to read in this one, so it scales best to 11x11. It's the most formal of the three.

C. Paper cut

Flat, matte, risograph-style colours with a faint paper grain, like regions cut out of coloured card. A huge condensed timer is the headline. Whimsy: queens are stickers that slap on at a slight random angle with a wobble, crosses look like pencil marks, and a solve shoots out paper confetti. The most playful of the three. The big timer pushes you to go fast, which you may or may not want.
